# IT Help Desk

## Overview

The IT Help Desk provides front-line technical support for all staff and students at Fictional Unified School District (FUSD). It handles account access, password resets, hardware issues, software installation requests, and general IT troubleshooting. Tickets are received via email, phone, and a self-service portal.

> **Note:** All names, contacts, and details in this page are fictional examples.

## SLA / Service Level Expectations

- **Uptime target:** not-applicable (staffed service)
- **Response time:** 4 hours during supported hours
- **Resolution time:** 1 business day for standard requests; 4 hours for critical issues

## Supported Hours

Monday through Friday, 7:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Closed on district holidays.

## Users / Audience

All FUSD staff and students. Approximately 5,000 end users across 12 fictional campuses.
